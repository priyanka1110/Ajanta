Our Services

Prosthetics

Advanced Prosthetic Solutions

We provide precision-engineered prosthetic systems for both lower and upper limb amputees — designed around your functional goals, activity level, and lifestyle.

Lower Limb: Advanced prosthetic knee systems including microprocessor-controlled, hydraulic, and polycentric mechanisms. Energy Storage & Release prosthetic feet for dynamic propulsion and shock absorption.

Upper Limb: Body-powered, myoelectric, and cosmetic prosthetic systems tailored for dexterity, grip functionality, and aesthetic restoration.

Clinical Goals

Stance stability Terrain adaptability Reduced fall risk Improved gait symmetry Dexterity enhancement Aesthetic restoration
Orthotics

Custom Orthotic Devices

Ankle Foot Orthosis (AFO): Advanced orthotic intervention designed to enhance ankle stability, improve gait mechanics, and prevent secondary deformities.

AFO Indications

StrokeCerebral PalsyFoot DropPeripheral NeuropathyMultiple Sclerosis

KAFO: Comprehensive stabilisation for patients with significant lower-limb weakness or neuromuscular compromise, with stance-control, carbon composite, and microprocessor-assisted options.

Spinal Orthotics: Custom spinal bracing for scoliosis, vertebral fractures, post-operative stabilisation, and degenerative spinal conditions.

Gait Optimisation Deformity Prevention Energy Efficiency Balance & Safety
Pediatrics

Specialized Pediatric Care

Children's prosthetic and orthotic needs are unique — their devices must accommodate rapid growth, high activity levels, and the developmental demands of childhood.

Our pediatric specialists work closely with families to create solutions that support every milestone: walking, running, playing, and thriving. Devices are lightweight, durable, and designed to grow alongside your child.

We manage congenital limb differences, cerebral palsy, spina bifida, and post-surgical rehabilitation across all pediatric age groups.

Pediatric Conditions We Treat

Congenital limb differences Cerebral Palsy Spina Bifida Club Foot Scoliosis Post-surgical rehab
Diabetic & Compression

Diabetic Foot & Compression Care

Diabetic Foot Preservation: Comprehensive pressure redistribution strategies utilising custom accommodative orthoses and specialised footwear to prevent ulceration, reduce infection risk, and preserve limb integrity.

Therapeutic Compression: Medical-grade compression garments for post-burn rehabilitation, lymphoedema, vascular insufficiency, and post-surgical management — designed for oedema control, scar modulation, and tissue stabilisation.

Ulcer Prevention Lymphoedema Post-Burn Rehab Scar Modulation

Patient Journey

The How It Works

From Consultation to Mobility

Every intervention at AJANTA follows a structured, outcome-oriented clinical pathway.

1

Initial Assessment

We sit down with you to evaluate your physical condition, review your complete medical history, and discuss your personal mobility goals. This is a comprehensive clinical consultation that forms the foundation of your treatment plan.

2

Digital Scanning & Casting

Using traditional plaster casting or advanced 3D scanning, we capture the exact contours of your limb or body for a flawless custom fit. Our biomechanical and gait analysis ensures every measurement is precise and purposeful.

3

Custom Fabrication

Our in-house technical team meticulously crafts your device using lightweight carbon fibre, flexible plastics, and high-grade titanium. Every component is selected and assembled with clinical precision to ensure optimal performance and durability.

4

Final Fitting & Trial

We perform a diagnostic check-socket fitting, making dynamic alignments while you walk or move, ensuring the final device feels like a natural extension of yourself. Functional optimisation continues until you are fully satisfied.

5

Long-Term Success & Post-Care

Our commitment doesn't end at fitting. We provide structured follow-up, outcome monitoring, gait training, adjustments, and repairs — ensuring your device performs optimally at every stage of your life. We are a trusted partner in your rehabilitation journey.
